Getting started

How do I connect my bank?

After you sign in, tap Connect with Plaid, pick your bank and log in. To add more banks later, go to Settings → Connect another bank.

Is it safe? Do you see my bank password?

No. Your login goes to Plaid and your bank, never to EZ Money. We only receive your transactions, the last 4 digits of each account number, and account names. There are no ads, and we never sell data. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Why don't I see older transactions?

EZ Money starts on the 1st of the month you connect a bank, so you begin with a clean, manageable stack instead of years of history.

Where are my transfers and credit card payments?

Money moving between your own accounts isn't spending or income, so EZ Money skips transfers and card and loan payments automatically. You can still find them under Activity → Ignored.

Sorting

I sorted something by mistake

Tap Undo in the bar that appears for a few seconds. Or go to Activity, tap the transaction and choose a different category. Changing a category is always free.

Can I make my own categories?

Yes. While sorting, tap More… → New category, or go to Settings → Categories. You can also hide the categories you never use.

What does "Regret it?" do?

It flags a purchase you wish you hadn't made. Your Summary shows how much your regrets add up to each month. It's just for you, no judgment.

Does EZ Money track income?

Yes. Paychecks are filed automatically, and other money coming in (like refunds or payouts) shows up as a card to sort. If you only care about spending, turn off Settings → Track income.

Free plan and EZ Money Pro

What's free?

Your first 50 sorts. A sort is filing a transaction into a category yourself. Ignoring, "Later", undoing and changing a category don't count.

How much is Pro?

$4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. Pro gives you unlimited sorting.

How do I cancel?

Apple handles billing. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → EZ Money and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

I got a new phone and lost Pro

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the plans screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds go through Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account

My bank says "Needs a fresh login"

Banks sometimes ask you to log in again. Go to Settings, find the bank, and tap Reconnect. Your sorted history stays.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. This erases all your data and disconnects every bank. It doesn't cancel a Pro subscription; cancel that first in your iPhone's subscription settings.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email us from the address on your account, and we'll send it within 30 days.
